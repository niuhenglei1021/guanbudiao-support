关不掉 Won't Stop

关不掉是一只闹钟。滑掉系统闹钟以后，铃声还会继续，直到你做完这只闹钟指定的动作。

Won't Stop is an alarm. After you slide the system alarm away, the ringtone keeps playing until you finish the action set for that alarm.

隐私 Privacy

闹钟保存在你的 iPhone 上。深蹲、俯卧撑、平板支撑、拍照和扫物体会使用相机。说一句会使用麦克风和语音识别。走路和摇动会使用运动数据。会员通过 Apple 购买，开发者收不到你的付款卡号。

Alarms stay on your iPhone. Squats, push-ups, plank, photos, and scanning use the camera. Saying a sentence uses the microphone and speech recognition. Steps and shaking use motion data. Membership is purchased through Apple. The developer does not receive your card number.
